The Concept

Ezuri Music is Steven’s new DJ concept: deep, percussive Afro and hypnotic Progressive House, electrified by the neon glow of the 80s — think Miami Vice aesthetics, Stranger Things ambience, and sonic nods to Journey and Depeche Mode.

Expect driving rhythms, emotional synth hooks, and a cinematic atmosphere that builds from twilight to midnight. It’s nostalgia reimagined for modern dancefloors — retro spirit, future energy.
